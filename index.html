3. Supplier Costs (COGS)

Add every cost your agency pays to suppliers. Choose how each cost is counted; the system calculates the line total and converts it into the quotation currency using your entered exchange rate.

How to fill each cost line: Supplier = who charges you; Expense = flight/hotel/tour/transfer/insurance; Cost basis = how the supplier prices it; Quantity = number of people/rooms/nights/groups; Unit cost = supplier price for one unit; Supplier currency = currency charged; FX rate = quotation-currency units for 1 supplier-currency unit (use 1 when currencies match). How pricing works

Traveller prices are added according to age category. Supplier costs are then compared against total selling price. The estimated profit is selling price minus supplier costs. Markup is a percentage added to supplier costs; the traveller prices are the package selling price, so avoid counting the same markup twice when setting your prices.

This prototype uses manually entered prices and does not fetch live FX rates or supplier prices.
